# First RBMK power: recipe and process dependency map

This is a source-level progression map for a **survival player reaching electrical power from a first RBMK**, including fabricated fuel, steam processing, and exhaust handling. It describes the current code, not a proposed recipe change. Quantities below are per recipe operation, not a bill of materials for a safe reactor layout. Recipe JSON overrides, modpack changes, and ore-dictionary substitutes can alter the actual in-game route.

## The main dependency graph

```text
Iron + carbon/coal -> steel -> plates, shells, pipes, structural parts
                            -> anvil tier 2 -> assembler
                            -> press + stamps -> plates and graphite

Uranium ore -> (centrifuge) uranium powder + Ra-226 byproduct
             -> (furnace) uranium ingot -> uranium billet -> NU RBMK fuel rod
Ra-226 + beryllium -> Ra-Be billets -> Ra-Be neutron-source rod
Depth zirconium ore -> zirconium nuggets -> ingots -> empty RBMK rod -> loaded rods

Optional enriched-fuel branch:
uranium powder + sulfuric acid + peroxide -> (chemical plant) yellowcake
yellowcake + fluorine -> (chemical plant) UF6
UF6 -> (gas centrifuge cascade) fuel-grade uranium nuggets
     -> fuel billets -> MEU RBMK fuel rod
fluorite + sulfuric acid -> (chemical plant) HF
HF -> (electrolyser) fluorine + hydrogen
ordinary water -> (chemical plant) fresh water -> (chemical plant) light water
fresh water -> (electrolyser) hydrogen + oxygen -> (chemical plant) peroxide

Assembler -> RBMK blank -> fuel column + control/absorber columns
RBMK ReaSim columns + light-water inlet -> super dense steam outlet
super dense steam -> turbine -> dense steam -> turbine -> steam
                  -> turbine -> low-pressure steam -> condenser -> fresh water
fresh water -> (chemical plant) light water -> RBMK inlet
                 power from turbines -> electrical load / power storage
```

Both 528 mode and forced ReaSim boilers default to **on**. In that mode all RBMK columns can boil water, and side-mounted ReaSim inlet/outlet blocks provide the fluid path. The ordinary RBMK boiler block is a **legacy-mode branch**, not a prerequisite for default ReaSim power. See [RBMKDials.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/rbmk/RBMKDials.java#L197-L204), [TileEntityRBMKBase.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/rbmk/TileEntityRBMKBase.java#L129-L169), and [GeneralConfig.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/config/GeneralConfig.java#L48-L51).

## Fuel and startup neutron source

1. **Empty cladding:** The grid makes 16 small empty rods from steel and lead `plate528`; four small rods combine into one quad rod. One empty RBMK rod needs a quad rod and six zirconium ingots. A loaded RBMK rod is that empty rod plus **eight billets** of one fuel type. [RodRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/crafting/RodRecipes.java#L39-L45), [RodRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/crafting/RodRecipes.java#L98-L104), [RodRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/crafting/RodRecipes.java#L322-L326).
2. **Shortest uranium fuel route (NU/UEU):** A normal uranium billet is the input to the `rbmk_fuel_ueu` recipe. Uranium ore can be furnace-smelted to uranium ingots; the mineral recipes convert ingots/nuggets to billets. This route bypasses yellowcake, UF6, chemical plant, and gas centrifuge for the fuel material itself. [SmeltingRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/crafting/SmeltingRecipes.java#L139-L140), [MineralRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/crafting/MineralRecipes.java#L168), [MineralRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/crafting/MineralRecipes.java#L692-L700).
3. **Fuel-grade MEU route:** The ordinary centrifuge can make uranium powder from uranium ore. The chemical plant consumes 4 powder + 1,000 sulfuric acid + 250 peroxide to make 2 yellowcake; it consumes 4 yellowcake + 600 fluorine to make 1,200 UF6. The gas centrifuge uses internal NUF6/LEUF6 stages. When a downstream centrifuge is processing LEUF6 and cannot transfer onward, its terminal code consumes 600 units from its output tank to make six uranium-fuel nuggets. Nuggets make uranium-fuel billets, and eight billets fill one RBMK MEU rod. [CentrifugeRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/CentrifugeRecipes.java#L272-L276), [ChemplantRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/ChemplantRecipes.java#L173-L187), [GasCentrifugeRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/GasCentrifugeRecipes.java#L20-L29), [TileEntityMachineGasCent.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/TileEntityMachineGasCent.java#L209-L224), [MineralRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/crafting/MineralRecipes.java#L202).
4. **Indirect MEU chemistry:** The chemical plant treats ordinary water into fresh water, then makes peroxide from hydrogen + oxygen and sulfuric acid from sulfur powder + peroxide + fresh water. The electrolyser splits fresh water into hydrogen/oxygen. For fluorine, the chemical plant makes hydrofluoric acid from fluorite crystal + sulfuric acid; the electrolyser splits HF into fluorine/hydrogen. [ChemplantRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/ChemplantRecipes.java#L2043-L2051), [ChemplantRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/ChemplantRecipes.java#L87-L99), [ChemplantRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/ChemplantRecipes.java#L1067-L1073), [ElectrolyserFluidRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/ElectrolyserFluidRecipes.java#L28-L29), [ElectrolyserFluidRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/ElectrolyserFluidRecipes.java#L68-L74). Thus the MEU path directly needs assembler, ordinary centrifuge, chemical plant, electrolyser, gas centrifuge, and the electricity/fluid network that runs them. It is not required by the NU recipe.
5. **Startup source:** Neither NU nor MEU rod has a nonzero self-neutron rate; `burn` starts from incoming flux plus that rate. A source rod such as Ra-226/Be has a positive self rate and is an explicit startup dependency for a fresh core. A Ra-Be billet uses radium and beryllium nuggets (or their billets); eight billets fill an RBMK source rod. The ordinary centrifuge's uranium-ore recipe also yields Ra-226 nuggets. [ModItems.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/items/ModItems.java#L4920-L4942), [ItemRBMKRod.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/items/machine/ItemRBMKRod.java#L140-L146), [ModItems.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/items/ModItems.java#L5201-L5214), [MineralRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/crafting/MineralRecipes.java#L257-L265), [CentrifugeRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/CentrifugeRecipes.java#L272-L276). This is a source-level startup inference; core geometry and neutron reach still need in-game verification.

The terminal fuel-feed step is performed by a **downstream gas centrifuge**, because a stage transfers enriched pseudofluid to the adjacent machine and terminal conversion checks the downstream machine's input type. Count at least two gas centrifuges for the code path, rather than assuming a single block does the whole cascade. [TileEntityMachineGasCent.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/TileEntityMachineGasCent.java#L147-L165), [TileEntityMachineGasCent.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/TileEntityMachineGasCent.java#L209-L224). The gas centrifuge itself is assembled from four centrifuge elements, stainless plate, steel tanks, rubber and an advanced circuit; each element requires titanium plate, stainless plate, a motor and rubber. [AssemblerRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/AssemblerRecipes.java#L207-L215), [AssemblerRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/AssemblerRecipes.java#L750-L759).

**The return path is not a direct pipe:** RBMK inlets/legacy boilers use `LIGHT_WATER`, whereas condensers output `FRESH_WATER`. The chemical plant's `WATER_DEMINERALIZATION` recipe performs the needed fresh-to-light conversion; initial fresh water comes from its `WATER_TREATMENT` recipe. `migrateFrom(WATER)` only reads old saved tank contents and is not an in-world fluid conversion. The recipe loop is present, but its plumbing, throughput, and actual steady-state operation remain unverified in game. [TileEntityRBMKInlet.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/rbmk/TileEntityRBMKInlet.java#L17-L19), [TileEntityCondenser.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/TileEntityCondenser.java#L37-L40), [ChemplantRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/ChemplantRecipes.java#L2043-L2060), [FluidTank.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/fluid/tank/FluidTank.java#L284-L310).

## Fabrication progression design brief (proposal, not implemented)

The desired gate is **manufacturing capability**, not an arbitrary recipe tier or the number of ingredients. Keep hand assembly and ordinary attachment at the crafting table. Introduce a machine step when the operation actually needs repeatable forming, welding/sealing, balancing, clean handling, heavy lifting, or automated scale. Every added station should serve a *family* of recipes; avoid a late-game machine whose sole job is to combine two finished items.

| Fabrication capability | Existing foothold | Appropriate work | RBMK-path examples to evaluate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hand tools and metalworking | Crafting table, tiered anvil, press, furnace/blast furnace. | Simple fittings, housings, plate forming, bolts, and joining finished interchangeable parts. | Keep ducts, small fittings, and simple column attachments hand-assembled unless their actual operation demands a process. |
| Early powered assembly | Current basic assembler, built at the tier-2 anvil. | Motors, circuits, compact machines, pumps and processing equipment needed to bootstrap power/chemistry. | It should still make the chemical plant, ordinary centrifuge, and the next fabrication station. It should not make a full RBMK structural blank merely because a template fits. |
| Heavy structural assembly | Existing assembly factory is a candidate, not already a tier: it currently shares the basic assembler recipe pool. Existing arc welder already makes welded plates and other welded products. | Large frames, shielded vessels, reactor columns, turbine casings and other heavy assemblies; substantial pre-nuclear power and fabricated structural parts. | Move the RBMK blank to this capability; its downstream ReaSim columns still use the blank. Consider whether large turbine bodies belong here, while leaving connections and control fittings simple. |
| Precision and controlled fabrication | Existing press, arc welder, centrifuges and chemical processing can supply substeps; a new reusable machining/balancing or fuel-fabrication capability is justified only after surveying its full recipe family. | Rotors and shafts requiring precision/balance; fuel pellet preparation, cladding, sealing and inspection where the operation is more than item combination. | Evaluate titanium turbine rotor, gas-centrifuge rotor/element, and RBMK/PWR fuel cladding together. Keep loading a finished fuel element into a finished column as a simple action. |

The **first candidate change** is to distinguish basic and heavy assembler eligibility and reclassify the RBMK blank; this makes a meaningful upgrade without inserting a contrived machine at each recipe. The existing assembly factory costs 48 steel ingots (in non-expensive mode), 18 motors, 16 basic circuits and other materials in a basic-assembler recipe, so it provides a concrete possible heavy-machine bootstrap. It currently provides six arms and uses fresh water while generating spent steam, but it can run the same templates as the small assembler; calling it an existing recipe tier would be inaccurate. [AssemblerRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/AssemblerRecipes.java#L3888-L3912), [TileEntityMachineAssemfac.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/TileEntityMachineAssemfac.java#L34-L52), [TileEntityMachineAssemfac.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/TileEntityMachineAssemfac.java#L151-L165), [TileEntityMachineAssemblerBase.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/TileEntityMachineAssemblerBase.java#L87-L106).

Implementation would need more than hiding a template in the UI: both machines resolve recipes in their common base class, and there is no recipe capability field today. Add explicit recipe eligibility with a backwards-compatible default for existing custom recipes, enforce it in server-side processing, and surface the requirement in template/NEI views. Preserve old output-identified and legacy-index assembly templates; do not make existing worlds' templates silently turn into a different recipe. The assembler recipe JSON currently stores output, inputs, duration and folders, so any capability field must be optional for old files. [TileEntityMachineAssemblerBase.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/tileentity/machine/TileEntityMachineAssemblerBase.java#L351-L372), [AssemblerRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/AssemblerRecipes.java#L5320-L5340), [AssemblerRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/AssemblerRecipes.java#L5359-L5413), [AssemblerRecipes.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/AssemblerRecipes.java#L5415-L5467), [SerializableRecipe.java](../src/main/java/com/hbm/inventory/recipes/loader/SerializableRecipe.java#L91-L124).

For crafting-table candidates, judge the **operation**, not the item's importance. A turbine rotor assembled and balanced from eight blades is a plausible precision-machining operation; a fuel rod's pellet loading and seal could be a reusable fuel-fabrication operation across reactor families. By contrast, placing a finished fuel item in an RBMK column, joining an absorber blank to its control fittings, or attaching a pipe is not a reason to demand a high-tier inserter. Before moving any grid recipe, check all alternative recipes, output counts, 528/expensive-mode branches, and whether the new machine can be built and powered *before* the product it gates.

The intended technology arc is: **hand metalworking -> starter non-nuclear power -> basic assembly and chemistry -> heavy/precision fabrication -> first NU RBMK and full turbine-water loop -> higher-throughput and enriched-fuel industry**. Fuel enrichment remains an upgrade to reactor fuel, not a hidden prerequisite for natural uranium. Reactor generation must not be required to manufacture the machines needed for the first reactor.

This document is based on source inspection only. No recipe changes, build, world launch, NEI check, fluid transfer test, or safe minimum core layout were performed. Before implementation, verify in game: a viable NU + source-rod geometry; the default ReaSim inlet's supply; the three turbine grades and condenser; and the chosen heavy/precision machine's complete bootstrap chain.
