## Reading the inventory

**A** physically representative; **B** simplified but defensible process identity; **C** misleading name/description or process identity; **D** fictional/nominal abstraction. Classification does not certify current energy/material conservation. A recognizable engine can be B while its numerical ledger is broken. A generic machine can contain B real recipes and D fictional ones; classify those processes separately. Recommended actions are future work, not changes in this pass.

The table records player meaning, internal class suffix (prepend TileEntity; oil/ and rbmk/ denote subpackages), inputs/outputs, amounts/cadence, electric/thermal work or output, limits, modifiers and physical judgment. **q** =0.5 J electrical; **TU** =0.5 J only where the thermal utility ledger actually applies; **HU/core heat/progress** remain uncalibrated. q/t ×10 =W at nominal 20 TPS. Stored electrical/heat capacity is never a nameplate W rating. “Recipe” means amounts and duration vary by the linked owner; selected full chain recipes are tabulated below, and selected declared buffer/time/consumption constants and source links are in the catalogue. Unspecified byproducts are recorded as omitted, not presumed zero mass/energy.

Scope includes acquisition, processing, manufacturing, power/heat conversion, storage/transport and nuclear/advanced source boundaries. Doors, communications, weapons crafting outside the industrial process ledger, decorative tiles and dummy/controller-only tiles are excluded; high-energy industrial beams/accelerators are included. DONOTMODIFY historical source is not current behavior. Loaded custom JSON, machine config, upgrades, dimensions, recipe mode and dials can alter every realized plant. Source arithmetic is an upper-bound/default model, not throughput measurement.
