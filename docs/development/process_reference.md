# Process reference

Current default implementation at 20 TPS. Enabled recipe JSON, IMC additions and server configuration can change the defaults. Production source and the loaded recipe maps decide actual behavior. Use the [documentation index](../index.md) for player guides and related references.

## Execution and accounting

`ProcessRecipe` validates and snapshots the bill. `ProcessBatch` reserves inputs and retains paid work, heat and products. `ProcessMachine` commits through the native inventory, tanks and electrical/qualified-heat sources on the owning server thread. Fractional species outputs accumulate in `MassRemainder`; they are neither rounded up into free output nor discarded. All deliverable products must fit. A blocked batch pauses with its state retained.

Chemical input + sensible input + electrical work + process heat = chemical products + sensible products + rejected heat. Chemical LHV bounds available energy; it is not a full formation-enthalpy database for salts, minerals or nonfuel ions. Nuclear mass defects and external irradiation terms are recorded separately from chemical accounting.

Recipe counts are species-specific: liquid litres, standard gas litres, water-equivalent steam, material items or specialist aliquots. See [fluid reference](fluid_reference.md). Shared physical reactor lanes are bounded by 8 kWe, 100 kWth, 12 liquid L/min and 6,000 standard gas L/min; water service has its own 60-L/min ceiling. Native machines can have different ratings. Displayed process ratings use maximum paid ticks, not storage size.

## Rotary drive

Defaults 1/2 ordinary counts/tick, 0.2/0.4 kg/s; saved fractional spent mass; at most 4 counts/tick in overrides. Steam expansion uses 40% of the ordinary-to-spent work (20/40 kW shaft); unused enthalpy is rejected. Fuel heat is separately paid at 1.8 MJ per output ingot-equivalent, at most 500 kWth and 85% fuel efficiency.

## Physical-model boundaries

Crystallizer crystal/concentrate assays, metal-electrolyser mineral/gangue chemistry, geological solid sources, reactor burnup and exotic energy remain explicit game models. Solvent/acid baths return carrier with integrated regeneration; a bath charge is not bulk per-item consumption. Repeating recipe identities cannot turn depleted isotope reject or spent lithium targets into fresh natural feed. Crystallizer efficiency saves work, never grants free retained solid feed. Cooling is finite: the Chemical Reactor has 200 kW rejection and the industrial reactor shares 1 MW across lanes.

For consumer nameplates and closed cooling, see [manufacturing reference](manufacturing_reference.md). Configuration and saved-state rules are in [compatibility](compatibility.md).
