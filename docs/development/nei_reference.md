# NEI process coverage and ownership

NEI views are read-only descriptions of native machine behavior. Recipe quantities come from loaded recipe registries or the owning implementation, with variable/environmental operations labeled explicitly. The [native machine catalogue](machine_catalogue.md) remains the general owner index.

All named HBM handlers are registered through `NEIRegistry`/`NEIConfig`; configured custom machines are registered individually. GTNH handler and catalyst IMC is client-only and uses registered item IDs. Informational handlers register their catalysts without constructing pre-post-load recipe snapshots.

## Reading the matrix

`Source R/U; GUI pending` means producer, consumer and machine-use paths exist in source. It is **not** a claim of a tested keyboard click or rendered GUI. R applies to actual material outputs; U applies to accepted input identities and machine catalysts. Outputless heat/work/service views deliberately have no fictitious R product. Filled containers and fluid icons navigate by fluid identity, independent of preview quantity/pressure/physical-mass NBT. Internal gas-centrifuge streams and typed ICF pellets retain their distinct identities. No automatic inventory transfer is promised by informational views.

The native-owner links below identify the tile and processing implementation. Registry/bill names in the model column identify the authoritative recipe source. Repeated hardware variants sharing one native tile remain one row; press/furnace/reactor owners are separate rows. The boundary inventory after the matrix covers support tiles which do not own independent resource recipes.

## Coverage disposition and navigation contract

The matrix contains **144 process/source owner families**, including **142 applicable processing or informational views**, one inactive legacy generator and one beam-driver boundary. There are **179 additional registered transport/control/structure/combat/decorative boundaries** with explicit exclusions. The anvil's material/tier aliases count as one family; multiple handlers and recipe pages do not count as additional machines. The autocrafter uses ordinary registered crafting rather than inventing a second recipe pool.

Seven handler classes were added: `SteamCycleHandler`, `FuelUseHandler`, `ExtractionHandler`, `NativeSmeltingHandler`, `ResourceProcessHandler`, `NuclearProcessHandler` and `UtilityProcessHandler`. Six existing views were rebuilt around native process descriptors: Rotary Furnace, Industrial Blast Furnace, gas centrifuge, Cryo, spent-fuel cooling and fusion. In total, 25 existing handler classes received fixes or navigation changes, alongside the shared matching/cycling and registration helpers. The preceding DRI/large-arc implementation is separate work and is retained.

Informational views identify production, consumption, utility requirements and conditional rates. They do not execute recipes, provide inventory-transfer overlays, simulate a world, roll drops, load chunks or manufacture energy items. A source/heat/propulsion service with no material product has a machine/input Uses entry and deliberately no fictitious Recipes output. An empty alias cannot become a free required ingredient or NEI fire placeholder. Retained hardware, utility charging, probabilistic yields and NBT-dependent operations are labeled explicitly.

Fluid icons and registered filled containers share fluid identity for navigation; quantities and physical-mass metadata are display data, not lookup identity. Gas-centrifuge internal streams have separate pseudo identities, and ICF pellets retain their fuel/muon identity. Steam quantities use **L water-equivalent**, with mass and native counts distinguished from vapor volume. Gases use standard L. Process work, thermal energy, electrical power and thermal power remain separate quantities.

Process descriptors are cached and indexed by item/fluid identity. Recipe/fluid registry revisions invalidate snapshots. The extraction view additionally compares its small configured bedrock-drop list and replacement flag, so the existing bedrock-drop command cannot leave an integrated client's cached table stale. Native machine configuration is otherwise loaded through the existing startup configuration lifecycle. Dedicated-client/server recipe and machine configuration files must agree; these views do not add a new server-to-client recipe/configuration synchronization protocol.

Oil controllers expose an optional, read-only `ExtractionProfile`; unknown addon subclasses may leave it absent. Research-reactor fuel and nuclear-furnace fuel definitions expose copied read-only snapshots. None of these accessors advances a machine or changes its processing state. Common fluid initialization uses a neutral registry revision rather than loading an NEI class on the server.
