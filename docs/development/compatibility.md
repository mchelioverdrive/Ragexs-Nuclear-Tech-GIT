# Configuration, persistence and compatibility

Current contracts for contributors and pack authors. [Generator reference](generator_reference.md), [fluid reference](fluid_reference.md) and native recipe loaders supply subsystem-specific details.

## Source-of-truth order

Production source plus loaded server configuration and enabled recipe overrides determine behavior. Current references describe stock defaults and explicit compatibility boundaries. Player guides simplify those defaults. Design proposals do not enable features. Historical audits record earlier evidence and cannot override current source or references.

Machine JSON values already written to a server are explicit configuration, even when they match an older default. Fresh default changes do not overwrite them. Underscore-prefixed recipe files are templates; enabled files retain loader validation and IMC boundaries. A physical recipe must declare the complete supported mass/work/heat bill. A legacy volume-only override cannot silently bypass that requirement. Existing 528/LBSM branches remain independent progression choices.

## Saved quantities and identities

- Preserve item, block, TileEntity, fluid and metadata identities. Append new fluid identities without reordering existing registrations; reject custom-name collisions.
- Reduced capacity limits new receiving. Retain saved over-cap contents and allow normal draining; never clamp, rescale or reinterpret unknown stock.
- Electrical storage uses half-joule `long` quanta. Versioned NBT writes `energyFormatVersion`, `energyQuanta` and applicable `energyCapacityQuanta`; unversioned legacy counts load exactly as quanta. Network state is not another energy store.
- Fluid identity determines quantity basis. Cryogenic liquid IDs remain liquid; their process-gas IDs are separate. Steam retains water-equivalent mass per count. Molten/nuclear/specialist aliquots keep their individual conventions.
- New reduced portable fills use `realSimContainerAmount`. Untagged old stacks keep historical quantities; unequal amounts must not merge as equivalent fills.
- Paid chemical fuel, process work, exhaust heat, fractional products and electrical storage are separate accounts. Display snapshots cannot recreate any of them.
- Source-specific Mini RTG items retain source, age and energy through crafting/placement/drops. Unidentified old Mini/Power units adopt Pu/Po once; unknown source NBT remains intact without generation.
- Material metadata 1500 retains magnesium; phosphorus is 1531; tin bronze is 51. No blanket conversion of indistinguishable old fragments or arsenic-bronze stock.

## Lifecycle and authority

World/inventory/fluid mutation belongs to the owning server thread. MachineRuntime lifecycle, dirty events, scheduled boundaries and native compatibility reconciliation own progress. Unload/replacement must not run deferred work against a new controller. Offline time produces no free electricity or process work.

Preserve multiblock proxies, native sided inventories, automation and computer controls. Client information follows server-owned status; matching client/server versions are required. Item previews use local settings. Keep client classes out of dedicated-server loading paths.

Legacy NBT machine status packets are decoded before being queued onto the client thread; tile lookup and status application happen there, as with buffer-based status packets. Machine information uses synchronized demand/draw snapshots without first evaluating the client's simulation getters. An unavailable recipe duration or zero configured display capacity produces an empty gauge, without changing machine contents. Strict mass/LHV validation remains required for simulation; unresolved preview properties omit only their exact physical calculation.

Fixed machine lane arrays retain their saved prefix and pad missing lanes with zero on load. The assembler and industrial assembler/chemical reactor use their current lane counts; steel furnaces and generators retain their fixed slot counts. Existing NBT keys and packet layouts are unchanged.

Heating-trait JSON must contain at least one step with positive input/output amounts and heat, and a registered output fluid. Cooling-trait JSON requires positive input/output amounts, nonnegative heat and a registered output. Invalid traits fail at configuration loading rather than being installed for tooltips or machine division. Filled output tanks block incompatible heat conversion. Watz segments with incompatible stored fluid types/pressures are not pooled; existing reaction and failure handling continue without that shared cooling operation. Recoverable over-cap stock remains stored.

Fluid settings copied to a machine, steam-type computer controls and the RBMK boiler grade selector require the affected tank to be drained before selecting a different type. Rocket Transporters retain an incompatible destination tank and skip that fluid transfer. Custom-machine production waits for compatible output storage, including already-reserved generator recipes. Mining Lasers recover 500 fluid units per oil/gas ore item only when the whole amount fits a compatible tank; blocked ore drops follow normal item collection, and merged stacks retain their remaining items. Industrial chemical reactor distribution weights only positive receiving room and bounds outgoing removal by available fluid.

Recipe display cycling can return no representative for an empty ore-dictionary entry; callers omit that unavailable item description. Universal NEI pages retain arrays of ingredient alternatives and reject missing required ingredient slots rather than dropping them. Optional outputs remain optional. Custom-machine pages retain their physical bill directly. Custom item-tooltip additions require a nonnull item belonging to the cached recipe; the normal parent tooltip is preserved otherwise, including GTNH NEI's per-visible-recipe callbacks.

## External boundaries

RTM retains the `hbm` mod/config identity and replaces upstream HBM in an instance. RF and legacy energy adapters convert at their existing API boundaries; their unit is not the new internal source of truth. Fuel LHV alone grants no heater, engine, aviation, turbine or propellant acceptance. OreDictionary aliases do not override exact native identity or exact item distribution.

The server-safe [RTMApi](../../src/main/java/com/hbm/api/RTMApi.java) identifies family `HBM`, variant `RTM`, API version 1. Optional integrations must remain usable with the other mod absent. Reactor HU/burnup, fictional sources and arbitrary pack scripts do not gain physical certification merely by connecting to an SI turbine or transport pipe.

## Wretch behavior, presentation and multipart geometry

The mature Wretch retains its feet-based origin, 3.2-by-20-block tracking envelope and server-safe `WretchPose`. Anatomical parts own damage targeting, including during ordinary dormancy. Native foot collision supplies stepping, gravity and support; the controller separately checks the raised torso and legs, with soft foliage yielding around them. This avoids treating empty space below the torso as a solid 20-block navigation volume. `WretchNavigation` replaces vanilla pursuit with bounded local surface A*, obstacle avoidance, short failure exclusions and accelerated steering. It never calls the vanilla distant pathfinder or requests chunks.

`WretchBrain` owns the sixteen encounter states, up to eight UUID-based memories, confidence ordering, disturbance attention and state-dependent movement. The original `EntityWretch.state()` values 0–7 retain their identities, value 8 is a structural strike, and values 9/10 add pull/assimilation. Health and saved encounter ownership remain compatible; grabs are favored and recovery is shorter. Health-based attack modifiers and livestock feeding are optional and disabled by default. Sight uses the rendered head and loaded rays; transparent glass is traversed one cell at a time, and a wall behind it still occludes vision. Physical attacks check their real articulated route through solid terrain. Ray helpers copy their origins because vanilla 1.7.10 block traversal mutates its input; enclosure rays and shared skeleton joints must not inherit those mutations. A lost target leaves a position memory rather than a continuously updated hidden entity reference. Timers advance only while loaded and admitted; save reload clears live targets/routes/captures and resumes safe searches.

The registered Wretch camp event listener supplies bounded movement, work, combat and native explosion cues to `WretchDisturbances`. Its world buffers hold at most 48 cues and 512 coordinate/UUID movement samples, never entity references. Native movement sampling spans ten ticks instead of relying on player `prevPos`, which can be reset before living-update events. Chunk/world unload clears or releases transient ownership. The existing camp registry supplies lifetime natural admission limits, retained reservations, player-placement protection and conservative new-chunk village provenance. Structural removal requires a meaningful current reason, real hand contact, both destruction settings as applicable, `mobGriefing`, no tile entity, native mining approval and an uncancelled Forge break event; removal revalidates the block and contact afterward. Without explicit player-construction permission, chunks lacking generation provenance fail closed even for natural-looking materials, preserving unknown historical player construction. No previous broad slam excavation or invisible pursuit-breaking path remains authoritative. See [configuration](../configuration.md#the-wretch) for limits and the legacy-placement boundary.

The client reuses its model, flesh texture, neutral detail texture and original audio. The full pose envelope controls renderer-side frustum culling. Metadata 17/18/19 carries behavior time, gesture time and the prior behavior; metadata 22 packs the behavior with its awake bit, retaining combat metadata 20/21 and the existing aim/capture fields. Integer metadata 31 avoids the 1.7.10 float terminator collision. Matching client/server builds are required. Embedded people remain bounded model geometry, without extra rendering entities; only anatomical collision parts are entities. Building gestures use the existing shared skeleton, and moving swipes/early reaches retain stepping. Charges blend their hunch into and out of locomotion.

`WretchCapture` owns one transient live victim, native collision-tested hand attachment, sneak/arm-damage interruption, a hold/pull/root-enclosure timeline and one checked root exposure. The live entity remains independent until native cancelable lethal damage/death succeeds; inventory/drop/respawn ownership is never transferred. A native death callback during that specific attempt permits a visual record only after all Forge listeners finish and its final cancellation state is checked; other deaths only release the capture. Chunk/world unload, logout and invalid ownership clear transient capture. Terminal Mockery replacement is deferred only while an actual Wretch owns that live host; the disease clock and all unrelated conversion behavior remain unchanged.

`WretchAnatomy` retains at most 12 completed UUID/seed/kind records, deduplicated on read and incorporation. Only seeds/kinds are sent to clients. Forge additional spawn data initializes the model and matching Wretch UUID; a bounded `WretchAnatomyPacket` snapshot updates current trackers after completion, on the client thread with connection/world/dimension/identity checks. Native entity position/velocity packets and player position correction synchronize the live captive. A cached MCP/SRG field access resets vanilla's block-only floating counter only while a real, collision-checked hand attachment remains valid, following the existing entity-support approach; no flight capability or server flight policy is changed; no client victim entity or inventory copy is created. The model preallocates seven baseline grafts plus twelve possible remains and reuses strand geometry. New remains vary position, scale and retained anatomy; only selected faces/fingers react at a time. Rendered roots progressively enclose the actual victim before the persistent fused anatomy appears. No completed record changes the authoritative body dimensions.

The primary references are Remy Abode's [Wretched Hands](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IXPN_skLYH8), [Old Bones](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9rSU54CyE4s) and [Christmas Eve Party](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gPIN2Q-PBQs), with the linked [Wretch wiki description](https://gemini-home-entertainment.fandom.com/wiki/Wretch) supplying the approximate two-telephone-pole scale. Bear origin, extreme skeletal limbs, asymmetric hands, the narrow skull, rooted pores and fused people inform the design. Twenty blocks, the complete unseen body layout, exact joints, graft placement/variation and individual motions are artistic interpretations. Window tapping, wall harassment and fused human tissue are reference-led; the sixteen states, sensing ranges, escape rules, lethal timeline, environmental exposure roll and optional feeding/health mechanics are gameplay adaptations. Awareness is suggested, not assigned uniformly to all embedded victims. The moving limb face is inspired by Glenn Arthur in Old Bones; other model grafts have no assigned identities. The original synthesized, wordless vocal effects do not identify a speaker or establish the source or agency of Mary's voice. All 27 mono Vorbis clips under `sounds/entity/wretch` were synthesized for RNT from oscillators and deterministic noise; no episode recordings or external audio samples are included. Heavy steps/impacts use greater native positional attenuation distances; quiet tissue and stalking effects use lower volumes and irregular server-owned cooldowns.

## Maintaining the documentation

Update the current reference when behavior changes; record the change in `CHANGELOG.md`. Preserve useful discovery evidence in the historical area rather than appending corrections to a contradictory current table. Keep exact source ownership and quantities here, operating explanations in gameplay, and candidates/unresolved choices in design. A source/build/static check is separate from in-game, visual, multiplayer or profiler acceptance.
