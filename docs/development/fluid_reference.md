# Fluid and chemistry reference

Current identity, phase and quantity contracts. [PhysicalRegistry](../../src/main/java/com/hbm/realsim/PhysicalRegistry.java) and the specialist constructors linked from [process reference](process_reference.md) own resolved mass/LHV. [PhysicalState](../../src/main/java/com/hbm/realsim/PhysicalState.java) defines 22,711,000,000,000 mass units/kg; unresolved is distinct from a known nonfuel with zero LHV.

`ProcessRecipe` snapshots exact integer masses; `MassRemainder` persists sub-count products. Liquid/gas/state conversion is by mass and paid work, never equal counts. Foundry shape quanta have no universal kilogram conversion. [Thermal reference](thermal_reference.md) owns steam enthalpy; [petroleum properties](substance_reference.md) owns the petroleum coefficient table.

The chemical LHV balance is an available-energy bound, not a complete standard-formation-enthalpy database for nonfuel ions and minerals. Utility values for compressed chemistry are adopted design envelopes. A zero nonfuel LHV does not assert zero formation enthalpy. Exact commercial reaction/cooling optimization is not claimed. Hot coolant transfer has its own explicit sensible bill; nuclear heat generation is a separate calibration boundary.

References distinguish identities/reference ranges from adopted game design: [DOE PEM targets](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/fuels/technical-targets-proton-exchange-membrane-electrolysis) support the adopted 55 kWh/kg-H2 envelope; [EPA chlor-alkali](https://www.epa.gov/sites/default/files/2020-09/documents/8.11_chlor-alkali.pdf) supports process identity; [NIST ammonia](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7664417&Mask=B) and [NIST osmium tetroxide](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/inchi/InChI%3D1S/4O.Os/q4%2A-2%3B%2B8) supply species identity; [USGS lithium](https://www.usgs.gov/data/lithium-deposits-united-states) distinguishes mineral/compound production from chloride electrolysis to metal. Assay choices, batches, capacities, rated flows and game module work above are RealSim design assumptions.


Pollucite processing adopts an **activated mineral oxide-equivalent assay** per kilogram: 15 wt% Cs2O equivalent, 3 wt% Rb2O equivalent, 10 wt% K2O equivalent, 10 wt% Na2O equivalent and 62 wt% aluminosilicate gangue. This describes a compressed activation/leach process, not a claim that natural pollucite is a mixture of free oxides. HCl supplies the chloride atoms. Mixed liquor is 1.15 kg/L; separated heavy/light liquors are 25 wt% mixed chlorides at 1.25/1.20 kg/L. Heavy salts dry with recoverable steam; calcium reduction yields molten CaCl2, returns unused Ca and vents its declared inert cover gas. No chlorine, sulfur or carrier water appears from an unrelated salt identity.


## Geological gas and laser fills

Fresh well/pumpjack/fracking gas is `RAW_NATURAL_GAS`: an adopted **helium-rich field assay of 98 mol% CH4 / 2 mol% He** at the standard-gas inventory reference. This is a RealSim resource assumption, not an average terrestrial natural-gas composition. Existing `GAS` remains pure methane and old stored gas retains its identity/amount. Both the reactor (600 standard L/min, 5 kWe) and cryogenic separator (6000 standard L/min, 50 kWe) can recover methane and helium without creating either element. The reactor recovery route supplies helium before space travel or an operating fusion plant.

Default primary gas-deposit output ceilings follow their retained source admission intervals: well 100 standard L per 200 ticks (600/min), pumpjack 200 per 50 ticks (4800/min). Crude ceilings remain 6/24/48 L/min for well/pumpjack/stimulation. Default stimulated oil coflow is 10–50 standard L per admitted oil litre; bedrock coflow is 1–5. Explicit geological gas-yield overrides retain standard-gas quantity semantics. Technical source buffers hold one parcel or one minute of the configured admitted flow. All paired outputs must fit before geological depletion or slurry injection. Incompatible old contents must drain before switching source product, and no source path clamps an old over-cap fill down to its new nameplate.

Laser fill mixtures are standard gas inventories, not isolated excimer compounds: N2/He premix 1:8, final CO2/N2/He 1:1:8; excimer component fills contain 99.8 mol% Kr or Xe and 0.2 mol% F2 or Cl2, with the final gameplay blend retaining equal input volumes. These are adopted fill assays, not claimed commercial laser optimizations. Mixing retains mass and gas chemical energy; actual excitation belongs to the laser's electrical work. Repeating molar fractions round only the per-inventory-unit mass coefficient down to the released mass quantum; fractional batch products remain owned in `MassRemainder`.

## Specialist quantities

These are explicit representations, not a universal litre rule. Counts and saved fluids are not rescaled. New normal liquids use reference litres: toluene 0.867 kg/L, glycerol 1.26, nitroglycerin 1.59; the organic sulfur-chlorine payload uses liquid identity 1.27 kg/L. Rocket fluid is an adopted 90-wt% hydrazine aqueous monopropellant, not a premixed oxidizer/fuel inventory. Chemical LHV is distinct from explosive detonation yield.

Mercury retains the native **125 aliquots/kg** convention. Fullerene is **1 g of 10-wt% C60 suspension per count**; recovery returns the other 90% as ligand. Cholesterol is a **1-g sterol-equivalent parcel**. Waste liquid, nuclear bromides and fluoride fuel salts are **1-g equivalent parcels/count**. They are nominal mass quantities, not gas/reference-liquid litres. Salt carrier LiF:BeF2 is 2:1 by mass, fresh salt 90% carrier/10% ThF4. Spent-salt/fuel packets are explicit game assays, not measured burnup/commercial reprocessing compositions. Radioactive solvent is an adopted 1-kg/L nonfuel aqueous working ligand with recoverable carrier, not a fabricated real molecular species.

Hot coolant is a 325-C pressure-maintained state retaining cold-reference counts. Adopted constant heat capacities are 2600 J/kgK for the existing alcohol/water coolant and 4200 for light/borated/heavy water. Delta T is 305 K. Pressure provides phase identity and changes no counted mass; no volume expansion is inferred. Qualified boiler input is 355 C; exchanger recovery is bounded by actual sensible difference and 2 MW. Heat and cold-return ownership persist separately until delivery. Cryogel carries an explicit negative sensible inventory and pays refrigeration work on creation.

Dairy adopts 3.5-wt% fat and 8.5% nonfat solids in 1.03-kg/L milk, with condensed carrier returned. Food energy outside declared fuel ingredients is uncalibrated; zero nonfuel LHV is not a nutritional measurement. Fictional blood/seed/gel/ligand mixtures conserve their declared input mass and energy without pretending to be real pure compounds.

Natural lithium irradiation adopts 7.59 atom% Li6, external captured-neutron mass and the Li6+n -> He4+T mass defect. T2 uses standard-gas quantities; remaining Li7, helium and subcount tritium stay in a non-reusable target. The captured neutron and mass defect are explicit external nuclear terms, not a chemical mass error or a second heat entitlement. Geological lunar He3 extraction uses a selected 1-ppm source assay, not an average soil claim.

Species/thermochemistry references: [NIST toluene](https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C108883&Type=HCOMBL), [NIST combustion compilation](https://www.nist.gov/publications/heats-combustion-and-related-properties-pure-substances), [IAEA isotope masses](https://www-nds.iaea.org/amdc/web/amdc_en.html) and [DOE lithium breeding identity](https://www.energy.gov/science/doe-explainsdeuterium-tritium-fusion-fuel). Densities, surrogate LHVs, grades, utility duties and machine rates remain explicitly adopted RealSim design values unless separately referenced.

## Shared pipe routing

Ordinary fluid ducts persist up to four unique `sharedPipeTypes`, retaining `type` as the first-channel compatibility field. Old saves with only `type` load as one channel. Each channel creates its own existing `FluidNode`/`FluidNetMK2` graph at the same coordinate; `UniNodespace` already keys nodes by coordinate and species provider. A single-fluid branch therefore joins only its selected graph. No fluid mixing, conversion, new transport budget or parallel transfer simulation is introduced.

Identifier clicks toggle one channel on the owning server thread. Changes detach all prior channel nodes, invalidate endpoints and queue loaded reconciliation. Valves detach all channels when closed; unload clears loaded references while retaining the existing world-owned dormant topology. Clipboard settings and description packets include the entire set. Shared ducts use the existing pipe models with connection geometry covering all assigned fluids; their color retains the first-channel view. Flow-gauge compatibility methods and display measure the first selected channel explicitly.
